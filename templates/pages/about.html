Our Purpose

What We Do

The Final Trip has taken a proactive step by establishing an organization that brings together all Cameroonian groups and volunteer associations dedicated to eliminating the need for public fundraising when a death occurs.

We urge every community member to join an association of their choice. We encourage them to avoid living in isolation and to be part of a group within the community. This can help prevent situations like those seen in Germany, where individuals' nationalities are often only discovered after their death, and they lack connections within the community.

Our Goals

The Objectives of Final Trip

01

Community Integration

Encourage members of the Cameroonian community to come out of their isolation by integrating the different existing associations in order to facilitate their integration and strengthen solidarity.

02

Sustainable Financial Solutions

Provide a lasting solution to the chapter of bereavement contributions with all the associations of the immigrant community in case of death of one of their members. The financial contribution during the loss of a member remains by far the most important help, and no one is ever quite prepared to assume the financial burden.

03

United Associations

Gather all existing Cameroonian associations in Germany to facilitate the operations surrounding the death of a member of the immigrant community in Germany.

04

Relieve Bereaved Families

By this socio-community approach, relieve the bereaved family and group by reducing the costs related to the death.

05

Collective Repatriation Management

Work collectively to facilitate the management of repatriation or execution of the last will of the deceased.

Integrity & Professionalism

Donor funds and associations are operated according to budget lines and all transactions are carried out transparently. The Final Trip never uses the funds for other reasons other than its primary objective.

We provide financial reports to donors and member associations, regardless of the amount. We believe that the timely submission of these reports creates the trust we earn from our donors.

Courtesy

All our volunteers are trained to treat all our stakeholders with respect, empathy, and professionalism at every interaction.

Fairness, Equity & Teamwork

We endeavor to reach out to all vulnerable and needy families regardless of their race, educational background, family history or color. We do not support political parties, religious groups or a particular group of the society.

We involve the grassroots in problem-solving. We take a facilitator role so that programs will be sustainable even in the event we wind-up programs in areas where we work.

Membership Conditions

One of the objectives pursued by Final Trip is to motivate each member of the community to attend an association of their choice. Persuade them out of isolation and belong to a group within the community to avoid cases like those that occurred in Germany in the past — people whose nationality was only discovered after their death, and whom no one in the community knew. It is often very shameful to deal with this kind of situation.

To be a member of Final Trip:

It is mandatory to be a member of an association or a group in the community.

The association or group of which you are a member or sympathizer is registered with Final Trip and indirectly grants you membership.

Any association in the community has the opportunity to offer all its members the right to be members of Final Trip by signing a membership agreement and payment of a one-time registration fee.
